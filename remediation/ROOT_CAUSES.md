## Development-trace investigation

The paired revision-1 development comparison used 16 seeds per configured condition. The selected sample-level diagnostic streams use seed `101`, combined noise, externally verified static startup, and startup bias calibration off. They are examples for mechanism inspection, not population estimates. Full selected traces, diagnostic summaries, and the shared input are in:

- `benchmarks/reports/pr2_revision1_development_diagnostic_traces_protocol_v2.csv`
- `benchmarks/reports/pr2_revision1_development_diagnostics_protocol_v2.json`
- `benchmarks/reports/pr2_revision1_development_imu_samples_protocol_v2.bin`
- Analyzer: `tools/pr2_development_diagnostics.py`

For `lateral_accel_0p2g`, `IMU_ONLY`, `LegacyHeuristic`, the seed-101 trace has 2,001 valid outputs and ends `Nominal`. It accepts 55 gravity updates during the 2–8 s lateral-acceleration interval; after acceleration stops, it rejects 36 attempted gravity updates from 8–12 s. Candidate tilt grows from `0.0396°` at 2 s to `14.2645°` at 8 s and `20.0450°` at 12 s. The end gyro-bias error components are `(-0.0024805, 0.0255409, -0.0211102) rad/s`. Across this trace, scalar tilt error has empirical correlations `(-0.584, -0.241, -0.596)` with x/y/z gyro-bias error; the logged x attitude/gyro-bias covariance correlation is `-0.835`. These indicate coupling but do not establish its direction of causality. Accepted gravity NIS mean is `1.4404`, p95 `3.2733`, max `11.3754`. Rejected gravity NIS and vector innovations are not emitted by the frozen evaluator, so they were not inferred.

For `pure_roll`, Conservative, the seed-101 heading-aid trace contains 11 accepted heading updates with NIS mean `1.4805` and maximum `6.6706`; zero were rejected. Aided tilt/yaw RMS is `1.6327°/2.1077°`, compared with `0.1214°/0.2362°` IMU-only. The per-seed effect is large, while the aggregate 16-seed result is smaller but still degraded. The trace schema stores scalar NIS, not the vector heading innovation; full innovation/covariance logging remains a next diagnostic step.

The root-cause investigation tested whether the finite-residual bias Jacobian explains all LegacyHeuristic behavior. It does not: after correcting that independent math defect, LegacyHeuristic yaw and gyro-bias regressions persisted on both development and disjoint confirmation seeds. This rules out that Jacobian mismatch as a sufficient explanation, but leaves the update/bias/covariance interaction unresolved.
